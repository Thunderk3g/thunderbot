## (a) Summary of the most important confirmations and corrections

**Corrections the report needs**

1. **Groww's ₹0 plan does allow order placement.** Groww's own blog (published 17 Sep 2026, modified 30 Sep 2026) says: "The difference between the free and paid plans is data access, not order placement. The Free Trial gives you Groww Cloud and every API except the Live Data and Backtesting APIs. You can authenticate, fetch instruments, place, modify and cancel orders, create smart orders, and check your positions, holdings and margin." The report left this open. A bot that gets prices elsewhere and only needs holdings, order status and (later) orders does not need the ₹499 plan, on Groww's own description. The plan is called "Free Trial"; no duration is stated.
2. **The "once per calendar week" quote is misattributed and not verbatim.** The NSE FAQ PDF the report cites (11 questions, 3 pages) does not contain it. The rule is in NSE circular NSE/INVG/67858 of 5 May 2025, Annexure A.6: "Clients will have the ability to update their mapped static IP addresses as needed, but not more than once a calendar week." The substance is right.
3. **DP charge basis.** The amount is right (₹3.50 depository + ₹16.50 Groww = ₹20 + 18% GST = ₹23.60). The report says "per ETF per sell day". Groww's help page says the opposite: "DP charges are applied per sell order, not per stock… Selling 5 units of Stock A in two separate orders → 2 DP charges". Groww also waives its ₹16.50 when the debit value is under ₹100. The 21 June 2025 start date is not shown on any Groww page read.
4. **Market orders.** NSE's rules treat every API order as an algo order and state "Algo orders with order type as Market Order are not permitted". The report does not say this. The bot should send LIMIT orders only. Groww's docs still list MARKET as an order type and do not explain how they reconcile the two.
5. **After-market orders.** The report says orders "must be sent during market hours". Groww's docs have no AMO flag, but every order response carries `amo_status` ("Status of the order placed after market. (Not applicable during market hours.)") with values NA, PENDING, DISPATCHED, PARKED, PLACED, FAILED, MARKET. The docs therefore imply after-hours orders are queued as AMOs; they do not say so in a sentence. Treat as "probably accepted, test with a small order", not "impossible".
6. **ETF price bands (outside the assigned sections, found on the way).** NSE circular NSE/CMTR/76101 of 1 Sep 2026 sets the new ETF parameters "w.e.f September 07, 2026", not 1 September. Bands differ by type: equity and debt ETFs 10% initial, flexed by 5% up to 20%; **gold and silver ETFs 6% initial, flexed by 3%; overnight and liquid ETFs a fixed 5%**. The report's blanket "initial band of ±10%" is wrong for GOLDBEES and LIQUIDCASE.

**Confirmed as the report states**

- STT on ETF units: 0.001% on the sell side for delivery; none on gold, liquid and gilt ETFs "and some international ETFs". Groww and Zerodha use identical wording.
- Brokerage lower of ₹20 or 0.1% per order, ₹5 minimum; stamp duty 0.015% on buys; SEBI fee 0.0001%; GST 18%; account maintenance ₹0.
- NSE transaction charge plus IPFT = ₹307 per crore (0.00307%) each side, from the NSE circular of 27 Feb 2026 effective 1 Mar 2026. The report's round-trip arithmetic (₹49.55, ₹94.28, ₹188.21) reproduces exactly on the confirmed schedule.
- Trade API ₹499 + taxes a month and ₹4,999 a year; `growwapi` 1.5.0 uploaded 6 Dec 2025 and still the latest; rate limits 10/s and 250/min for orders, 20/s and 500/min for non-trading calls; token endpoint and both auth mechanics; "Expires daily at 6:00 AM".
- The 150-token-requests-per-24-hours cap and the 8–20 character reference-id rule, which the report had only from repo comments, are both in Groww's docs.
- SEBI: framework applies to all brokers from 1 April 2026 (SEBI circular of 30 Sep 2025, para 8). No later SEBI circular with "algo" or "algorithmic" in the title exists on SEBI's circulars listing as of 10 Nov 2026. NSE re-issued the static-IP standards unchanged in its consolidated circular NSE/INVG/73992 of 30 April 2026.
- Static IP: primary plus optional secondary; one client per IP except family; 10 orders per second threshold; Zerodha's "regardless of your order rate", "ISP… cloud provider (such as AWS or GCP), or a VPN/VPC service" and "may lead to suspension" quotes are all verbatim.
- Reads are exempt from the IP check: now supported by exchange text (NSE FAQ: "Client static IP will be required only in case of Tech savvy Investor using API for placing orders") and by Zerodha explicitly. For Groww it is implied, not stated.
- Tax table rows for equity, gold/silver and debt ETFs match Groww's ETF tax article (modified 27 July 2026). The 87A inference is confirmed for FY 2025-26 by Freefincal.

**Still unconfirmed**

- Whether code deployed on Groww Cloud needs no client static IP. No Groww page read says so.
- Groww's own IP change limit, the error returned for an unregistered IP, IPv6 acceptance. Groww's developer docs contain no mention of static IP at all; their changelogs stop in December 2025.
- Income-tax Act 2025 section numbers, section 194K, international ETF holding period, gold ETF units bought April 2023 – March 2025. incometaxindia.gov.in returned 403 twice; AMFI's site has no tax page in its sitemap.

## (c) New facts worth adding

**What Groww Cloud is.** "Groww Cloud" is the brand of the logged-in API console at groww.in/trade-api. Its pages are titled "Groww Cloud | API Keys" and "Groww Cloud | Strategies"; the Strategies page describes itself as "Upload and deploy your trading strategies to Groww Cloud. Create and manage your algorithmic trading strategies with our powerful platform." ([Strategies page](https://groww.in/trade-api/strategies), fetched 10 Nov 2026). Groww's blog describes the workflow: "You write and deploy strategies from the API console, which also holds your API keys and documentation. Open the Strategies section from the left rail… Click 'Add new strategy', or open the sample strategy that is already listed. The Script tab holds the code, and the Requirements tab holds the packages your script needs… The status next to the code shows whether the strategy is Active or Inactive. Use 'Run now' to run the script once, or 'Save & deploy' to deploy it." The sample "sets up the API with your key and secret, places a market Buy order for a defined quantity, waits, and then places a Sell order." ([Groww blog](https://groww.in/blog/api-trading-on-groww), modified 30 Sep 2026). It is included in the ₹0 Free Trial plan.

Not stated anywhere read: the language (Python is implied by the SDK and a "Requirements" tab), any scheduler or cron, run-time limits, outbound internet access (for example to Supabase), price beyond the plan table, and **whether deployed code is exempt from the client static-IP requirement**. Groww's static-IP article hints at a managed alternative ("While static IPs are essential for compliance, setting one up can involve extra effort, cost, and ongoing maintenance… Want Full Control? Here's How to Set Up a Static IP — If you prefer managing your own setup") but never names it. The console's text lives in JavaScript bundles on `assets-netstorage.groww.in`, which was not on the allowed host list and was not fetched.

**The exchange rules cut both ways on Groww Cloud.** Supporting it: NSE A.5 says "For the broker-generated algos, the static IP shall be that of the broker or the client", and FAQ Q10 says that for a platform "hosted on Trading Member's infrastructure… it would be the static IP of the Trading Member's server". Against it: FAQ Q5 says "Tech Savvy client is required to host the Algo using a static IP at their end where Algo logic resides instead of hosting the Algo on Trading Member's cloud server." ([NSE FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQ_Retail%20Algo_03112025_NSE.pdf); [NSE/INVG/67858](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INVG67858.pdf)). How Groww classifies a client's own script running on Groww Cloud is not published. Also note the sample uses the key-and-secret flow, which needs daily approval; an unattended deployment would need the TOTP flow.

**Market orders are barred for algo orders.** NSE FAQ Q11: "As per point 8.1.1.12 of NSE circular NSE/MSD/67753 dated April 29, 2025, 'Algo orders with order type as Market Order are not permitted'". Operational modalities, market price protection check: "Members are required to adhere to the Market Price Protection check, by not placing any algorithmic orders on the Exchange as a market order." Restated at 8.1.12 of the 30 April 2026 consolidated circular. Since "all orders received via API from clients… shall be considered as Algo", API market orders reach the exchange, if at all, as price-protected limit orders at the broker's discretion. ([NSE FAQ](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/sites/default/files/inline-files/FAQ_Retail%20Algo_03112025_NSE.pdf); [operational modalities](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INVG69255.zip); [consolidated circular](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/INVG73992.zip)).

**Other limits from Groww's blog** (30 Sep 2026): "250 order requests per minute / 300 market data requests per minute / 300 OHLC data requests per minute / 3,000 real-time subscriptions per user / 25 modifications per order / 5 sessions per user / An average response speed of 65 ms". The docs say "For Live feed, upto 1000 subscriptions are allowed at a time", so the two disagree on subscriptions. ([blog](https://groww.in/blog/api-trading-on-groww); [docs](https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/python-sdk)).

**Groww's developer docs are stale.** The Python changelog ends at "[1.5.0] - 3rd December, 2025" and the cURL changelog at "User Profile API - 2nd December 2025"; neither mentions the static-IP regime that began on 1 April 2026. "Deprecated endpoints receive 6-month sunset notice." ([Python changelog](https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/python-sdk/changelog); [cURL changelog](https://groww.in/trade-api/docs/curl/changelog)).

**DP charge handling for the bot.** Because Groww describes the charge as per sell order, an exit from one ETF should be a single order, and a partially filled limit order that is re-entered as a new order may attract a second ₹23.60. ([Groww help](https://groww.in/help/stocks/searchable/are-dp-charges-per-stock-or-per-order)).

**GST applies to the API fee.** ₹499 + 18% = ₹588.82 a month; ₹4,999 + 18% = ₹5,898.82 a year (arithmetic on Groww's stated rates). ([pricing](https://groww.in/pricing); [blog](https://groww.in/blog/api-trading-on-groww)).

**Zerodha details useful for comparison.** Static IP configuration is per developer account; the family declaration reads "I confirm that the above static IPs will be used exclusively by me and/or my immediate family"; maximum order rate "10 orders per second (OPS) per client (trading) account". Zerodha's FAQ says the requirement runs "from 1 April 2025" while its static-IP article says "effective 1 April 2026"; the SEBI circular supports 2026. ([Zerodha FAQ](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/kite-connect-api-faqs); [Zerodha static IP](https://support.zerodha.com/category/trading-and-markets/general-kite/kite-api/articles/static-ip)).

**ETF price-band framework, operative date and bands** (report section "Practical traps"; unconfirmed-table row "Operative date of SEBI's ETF price-band framework"). NSE/CMTR/76101, 1 Sep 2026: parameters "applicable for Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) w.e.f September 07, 2026", following SEBI circulars of 15 June 2026 and 28 August 2026 (the latter an extension, forwarded by NSE/CMTR/76041). "Base price… shall be T-1 day Closing Price i.e. last 30 minutes of Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) of the ETF"; with no trade in the last 30 minutes, the day's last traded price; with no trade that day, "the latest available closing NAV". Bands: "Equity ETFs and Debt ETFs (other than Overnight ETFs and Liquid ETFs): Dynamic price bands, with an initial price band of +10%. The price band would be flexed by 5% of the base price after the cooling off period up to +20%. Overnight ETFs and Liquid ETFs: Fixed price band of +5%. Commodity ETFs (Gold/Silver): Dynamic price bands, with an initial price band of +6%. The price band would be flexed by 3% of the base price, after a cooling off period." (The text extraction shows "+"; the PDF very likely prints "±".) Gold and silver ETFs also get a pre-open session. ([NSE/CMTR/76101](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR76101.pdf); [NSE/CMTR/76041](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR76041.zip)).

**Budget 2026 items touching these instruments, beyond STT.** Freefincal reports the Finance Bill 2026 change that "no deduction shall be allowed in respect of any interest expenditure incurred for earning dividend income", and that secondary-market buyers of Sovereign Gold Bonds "must pay capital gains tax… from '1st day of April, 2026… tax year 2026-27'". Neither changes ETF capital-gains rates. ([Freefincal dividends](https://freefincal.com/budget-2026-stock-and-mutual-fund-dividends-to-be-taxed-without-interest-deductions/); [Freefincal SGB](https://freefincal.com/budget-2026-sov-gold-bonds-tax-free-only-for-original-subscribers-if-held-to-maturity/), both 1 Feb 2026).
